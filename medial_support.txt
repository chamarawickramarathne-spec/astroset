# AstroSet - Marketing Support

## Application Title
AstroSet - Daily Cosmic Events & Weather

## Short Description
Your daily guide to Vedic and Western astrology, planetary alignment charts, moon events, solar activity, space weather, and forecasts - all in one beautiful Android app.

## Long Description
AstroSet is a comprehensive daily events application that brings together astrology, planetary tracking, solar activity, space weather, and terrestrial forecasts to keep you informed about the cosmos and your environment. Available for Android mobile.

**Astrology Events:**
- Interactive Planetary Alignment Chart - a zodiac wheel showing live positions of all 10 planets (Sun through Pluto) with degrees, signs, and retrograde markers
- Daily Vedic Panchang (tithi, nakshatra, karana)
- Western daily horoscopes
- Moon phases with precise illumination percentage and age
- Upcoming Moon Events - full moons, new moons, quarters, perigee and apogee dates for the next 30 days
- Upcoming Planetary Events - retrograde stations and sign ingresses for the next 30 days

**Solar & Space Weather:**
- Real-time solar flare detection from NOAA GOES-18 X-ray data with class magnitudes (C, M, X)
- 7-day X-ray flux area chart with flare-class threshold lines on a log scale
- Geomagnetic storm tracking (G1-G5 scale)
- Kp index with severity indicators
- Aurora visibility forecasts
- Live solar wind speed, density, temperature, and IMF Bz (GSM) with sample timestamps
- Schumann Resonance monitor - live Earth-ionosphere spectrogram from Tomsk State University plus a composite Activity Index (0-100) with plain-language interpretation

**Weather Forecasts:**
- 7-day detailed forecasts
- UV index with safety level indicators
- Precipitation chances and wind speed
- Hourly forecast details

**Smart Features:**
- Automatic refresh every 30 minutes with countdown
- Offline caching - your last data is always available, even without internet
- Automatic over-the-air updates - new features and fixes reach you instantly through EAS Update, no manual downloads needed
- Your settings are saved - location, zodiac sign and preferences persist between sessions
- Custom location settings with latitude/longitude
- Clean dark-space themed interface
- Pull-to-refresh on mobile
- Built-in Privacy Policy screen

AstroSet is designed for astrology enthusiasts, stargazers, aurora hunters, photographers planning golden-hour shoots, health-conscious users tracking UV exposure, and anyone curious about the connection between cosmic activity and daily life. Planetary positions and moon events are computed on-device with precision astronomy algorithms, while live space weather comes from NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center and NASA, weather from Open-Meteo, and horoscopes from dedicated astrology services. AstroSet is completely free to use with no ads.
